# Lines for the demo video

Short lines to say out loud. Each one is true of what is in this repo.

- "Imani can't force a seller to open up. It makes opening up cheap, and it makes a refusal visible."
- "We tested it 66 ways: 14 honest auctions that must pass and 52 with a cheat built in that must fail."
- "Every one of the 27 checks has a cheat that makes it fail, so none of them is for show."
- "A second checker, written separately in Python, agrees on every round it can decide."
- "We re-read a real round from another project, Blind Bazaar, and got the same winner and price they published." (Say "re-read", not "audited": refunds are outside what their key shows.)
- "A bidder can check their own bid with just their own key: `imani bidder-check`."
- Show the ZODL scan of the mainnet bid link.

Don't say: that we read other projects' auctions in general, or that Imani is the most tested entry in the track.
